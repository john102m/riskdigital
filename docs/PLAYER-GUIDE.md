# Risk Digital — How to Play (Quick Guide)

## What is it?

A digital version of the board game Risk. The TV shows the world map. You play on your phone.

**Goal:** Complete your secret mission (or conquer the world).

---

## Getting Started

1. Open the link on your phone's browser
2. Enter your name, pick a colour and avatar
3. Create a game or join with the code shown on TV
4. Host can add AI players and starts the game when everyone's in

---

## Game Flow

### 1. Initial Placement

Territories are dealt randomly. You take turns placing one army at a time on territories you own. Keep going until everyone's placed all their armies.

**Tip:** Stack armies on border territories where you'll be attacking or defending.

### 2. Your Turn (repeats until someone wins)

Each turn has 3 phases:

#### 🟢 Reinforce
- You get bonus armies based on: territories owned ÷ 3 (minimum 3) + continent bonuses
- Tap a territory to place one army, or tap **All** to dump all remaining on one territory
- Place all your armies, then tap **Done → Attack**

#### 🔴 Attack
- Pick one of your territories (with 2+ armies) to attack FROM
- Pick an adjacent enemy territory to attack
- You have two options:

**⚔️ Attack (single roll)**
- You roll up to 3 dice, defender rolls up to 2
- Highest dice are compared in pairs — defender wins ties
- You lose armies or they do (1-2 per roll)
- You stay in control — you can stop any time, keep your remaining armies

**⚡ Blitz (all-or-nothing)**
- Auto-repeats dice rolls until you either capture the territory or are left with 1 army
- Much faster, but you CAN'T STOP once you start
- If the odds are close, you might burn through all your armies and end up with 1 left
- ⚠️ Blitz uses max dice available — if you only have 2 armies, that's 1 die vs their 2. You'll likely lose!

**When to use each:**
| Situation | Use |
|-----------|-----|
| You have 15 armies vs their 3 | ⚡ Blitz — easy win, save time |
| You have 8 vs their 6 | ⚔️ Dice — you might want to stop early if it goes badly |
| You just want to weaken them | ⚔️ Dice — roll a few times then stop |
| You need the territory for your mission | ⚡ Blitz — commit fully |

- After capturing, you **must** move troops into the new territory:
  - Use − / + buttons to choose how many (or tap **Max**)
  - Minimum = number of dice you rolled (e.g. 3 dice = must move at least 3)
  - Maximum = all but 1 from your attacking territory (you always leave 1 behind)
  - Tap **Move Troops** to confirm
- Attack as many times as you want, then tap **Done → Fortify**
- You earn a card if you captured at least one territory this turn

#### 🔵 Fortify
- Move armies from one territory to an adjacent one you own
- Pick source territory (collapses to green chip) → pick target → choose how many with − / + / **Max**
- One move only per turn
- Tap **Fortify & End Turn** to move and end, or **Skip → End Turn** to skip it entirely

## Your Phone Screen

### When it's NOT your turn
- Screen shows the current player's name in their colour + what they're doing
- Just wait — it'll switch automatically when it's you

### When it IS your turn

**Top row:**
- 🎯 (top-left) — tap to view your secret mission
- Phase badge (centre) — shows which phase you're in + info (e.g. "Reinforce · 5")
- 📊 (top-right) — tap to see mission progress + continent breakdown
- 🃏 (if you have cards) — tap to open/close your card hand

**Territory lists:**
- Grouped by continent (coloured headers)
- Tap a continent header to open/close it
- Tap a territory button to select it or place an army there
- Number on each button = armies you have there

**After selecting (Attack/Fortify):**
- Your chosen territory shows as a green chip at the top
- Tap ✕ on the chip to change your selection

**Bottom button:**
- Main action to advance the phase (e.g. "Done → Fortify", "Skip → End Turn")

---

## Cards

- Earn one card per turn (if you captured a territory)
- Trade sets of 3 for bonus armies: must be all-same type OR all-different
- Fixed values: Infantry = 4, Cavalry = 6, Artillery = 8, One-of-each = 10
- **Must trade** if you have 5+ cards at the start of your turn
- Tap the 🃏 badge to see/trade your cards

---

## Missions

- You get a **secret mission** at the start (tap 🎯 to view it)
- Complete it to win! (e.g. "Control Europe and Australia" or "Own 18 territories with 2+ armies each")
- Tap 📊 for progress
- Don't tell anyone your mission!

### All Mission Cards (14 total)

**Continent Conquest (6):**
- Control North America + Africa
- Control North America + Australia
- Control Asia + South America
- Control Asia + Africa
- Control Europe + South America + any third continent
- Control Europe + Australia + any third continent

**Territory Count (2):**
- Own 18 territories with at least 2 armies on each
- Own 24 territories (any army count)

**Elimination (6):**
- Eliminate the Red player
- Eliminate the Blue player
- Eliminate the Green player
- Eliminate the Yellow player
- Eliminate the Purple player
- Eliminate the Orange player

*If your elimination target is knocked out by someone else, your mission becomes: conquer the world (own all 42).*

## Key Rules

- **Defender wins ties** on dice
- **Must leave 1 army behind** — you can never empty a territory
- **Locked attack front** — once you attack from somewhere, you must keep attacking from that territory or territories you've just captured (can't jump to the other side of the map mid-turn)

---

## Tactics & Tips

**Early game:**
- Grab a small continent ASAP — Australia (2 bonus, 1 entry point) or South America (2 bonus, 2 entry points) are easiest to hold
- Don't attack everywhere — focus on one area and stack the borders
- Leave interior territories at 1 army (they can't be reached)

**Mid game:**
- Count your reinforcements vs opponents — continent bonuses are huge. Deny them theirs by holding one territory in their continent
- Trade cards as late as possible (unless forced) — the bonus gets bigger each time anyone trades
- Earn a card every turn — even a single easy capture is worth it just for the card

**Combat maths:**
- 3 dice vs 2 dice: attacker wins ~60% of the time on average
- You need roughly 1.5x the defender's armies to have good odds of capturing
- Defender wins ties — so even fights slightly favour the defender

**Late game:**
- If someone's close to their mission, you need to block them (capture a territory in "their" continent)
- Don't overcommit to one attack — you might need armies to defend elsewhere
- Watch card counts — a player with 4-5 cards who captures someone gets a massive trade-in

**General:**
- Be unpredictable — if everyone knows where you're heading, they'll gang up
- Sometimes the best move is to NOT attack and just stack up for next turn
- Keep your mission secret — don't make it obvious what you're going for
